Full-day pontoon → Shell Island
Pick up the pontoon (typically launches 9am). Cruise ~15–20 min across Grand Lagoon to Shell Island — a protected barrier island with no cars, white sand, and clear shallow water. Anchor on the bay side, wade to the beach, snorkel, play games on board, explore. You may spot dolphins on the way. Bring the snorkel gear, a bluetooth speaker, and sunscreen.

🤿 Shell Island Tips
Anchor on the bay side (calmer) and walk across the narrow island to the Gulf side for the clearer snorkeling water. The snorkeling is best near the jetties at the eastern tip — look for sand dollars, crabs, stingrays, and small reef fish.

Bring reef-safe sunscreen, water shoes (the shells can be sharp), and a portable anchor.

St. Andrews State Park
Best beach in the area — $8/car, way less crowded than the strip. Tide pools, calm kiddie cove, great snorkeling near the jetty. Bring your own gear since you already have it. Hang out for the afternoon.
🏞️ St. Andrews
7:00 PM
Dinner — McGuire's Irish Pub
A PCB institution. Huge portions, great steaks, live music, fun atmosphere. Works great for a big group. Known for their ribeyes and house-made root beer. Make a reservation — it gets packed.
